# 2026-08-20 — 0.2.5: pipelined readback built, and an honest negative result

**TL;DR:** the "double-buffered readback" optimization (candidate #1 from the
0.2.4 plan) is implemented, unit-tested, and A/B-profiled on the dev machine —
where it turned out to make **no measurable difference**. The measurement
explains why, the feature stays in behind an ini gate (off by default), and
the finding sharpens the case for the GPU-only path as the real fix.

## What was built

- `capture_core/readback_ring`: a small slot scheduler (fourth unit-tested
  doctest suite in the project, 6 cases) that tracks which of two cached
  system-memory surfaces receives this tick's `CopyRects` and which one —
  filled a full capture interval earlier — is safe to `LockRect` without
  waiting on in-flight GPU work.
- `proxy/frame_capture.cpp` now keeps two cached readback surfaces and, in
  pipelined mode, locks the previous tick's surface (one capture interval of
  extra overlay latency, ~11 ms at the default rate cap). Debug BMP frames
  always capture synchronously, so BMPs still show the exact frame and work
  on the priming tick after a device reset.
- Gate: `[VR] PipelinedReadback` (default 0). The `[xiii-perf]` heartbeat now
  reports the mode as `pipe=0/1`, so any pasted log is self-describing.

## The A/B result (dev machine, Amos01, windowed 1280x960)

| Mode | copy avg | copy max | lock avg |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `pipe=1` (pipelined) | ~3.3–4.5 ms | ~4.5–26.5 ms | ~0 |
| `pipe=0` (synchronous) | ~3.3–4.3 ms | ~4.8–15.4 ms | ~0 |

No difference, and `lock` was already ~0 in both modes. The interpretation:
on this machine's driver, the wait happens **inside `CopyRects` itself** —
the driver synchronizes with the GPU at blit time, before returning. The
optimization was designed to stop the *lock* from waiting on an in-flight
copy, but nothing here ever waited at the lock in the first place. Alternating
destination surfaces cannot remove a stall that lives in the copy call.

This was flagged as a risk before writing any code (the 0.2.4 profile already
showed `lock ~0`), which is why the change went in behind a gate with the A/B
telemetry, rather than as a replacement for the verified path.

## Why the gate stays in

Driver behavior differs: another driver may queue the blit and defer the
wait to `LockRect` — exactly the case pipelining fixes. The home machine
(NVIDIA) can settle this with a one-line ini flip and two heartbeat samples.
The default stays off, so an untouched install behaves exactly like the
hardware-verified 0.2.4.

## Consequence for the plan

If the home machine's A/B also shows the stall inside `CopyRects`, the only
remaining lever that *removes* the cost (rather than moving it) is the
GPU-only shared-surface path: share the D3D8 backbuffer with the D3D11
device and feed `SetOverlayTexture` without any CPU round-trip. That is the
hardest of the candidates, which is why the home numbers should confirm the
cheap options are exhausted first.
